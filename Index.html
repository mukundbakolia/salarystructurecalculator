Statutory rates used: EPF 12% (employer + employee, PF wage capped at ₹25,000 by default), ESIC 3.25% employer / 0.75% employee (wage ceiling ₹21,000/month), gratuity 4.81% of Basic, statutory bonus 8.33% (bonus-wage capped ₹7,000). Basic is always the greater of (Basic % × Gross) or the UP minimum wage for the selected category. Professional Tax and Labour Welfare Fund are not levied in Uttar Pradesh and are off by default.

This is a planning tool, not a compliance or tax filing system — verify current thresholds with the latest EPFO/ESIC circulars and the UP Labour Department's notification before issuing an offer.
